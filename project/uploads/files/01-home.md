## Section: Building Character for Christ
At Brooklyn Seventh-day Adventist School, we strive to help students develop into well-rounded individuals by fostering their social, mental, physical, and spiritual development. We recognize the power of positive character building and work to instill in our students with values that reflect the world of Christ. We create an environment where students feel safe and secure and learn to appreciate the wisdom and love of Jesus.

## Section: Fostering Mental and Physical Development
We strive to provide our students with the best educational experience possible, in order to help them reach their full potential. We recognize that the first years of school are the most important and intensive learning years in a child's life, and work to provide students with the skills necessary for high school and beyond. Through a balanced curriculum of physical activities, creative arts, and academic subjects, we seek to foster our student's mental and physical development.

## Section: Developing a Culture for Service
At Brooklyn Seventh-day Adventist School, we believe in the importance of developing a culture of service and care. We strive to equip our students with the skills and knowledge necessary to become active members of the community and teach them the importance of giving back. We emphasize the importance of compassion, respect, and responsibility, and work to cultivate a spirit of service in our students.
