## Admissions Process
Brooklyn Seventh-day Adventist School is committed to maintaining a diverse student body. Accordingly, we welcome applications for admissions from girls and boys of all races, ethnicities, and economic backgrounds. Admissions decisions are made on the basis of a candidate's individual strengths and talents, potential to thrive, and ability to contribute positively to our community.

### Apply
1. Complete the application form.
2. Pay Application fee ($60 – nonrefundable)
3. Meet with Administration/Principal (Virtually or In-Person)
4. Placement Test (Nov 12, Jan 21, Mar 17, May 19, Jul 21, Sep 8 & 15)
5. Status of Application (accepted or denied) — if accepted, proceed to step 6.

### Enroll & Pay
15. Scheduled Registration Periods: PreK & K (Aug 19, 2024) / Grades 1&2 (Aug 20) / Grades 3&4 (Aug 21) / Grades 5&6 (Aug 22) / Grades 7&8 (Aug 23)
16. Sign contract, pay fees (class dues, book fee, home and school, technology, yearbook, insurance fees, choir dues, music class fee, stem class fee, graduation fee, after-school program, sports dues, club dues), 1st and last month's tuition.

### Do it Quickly
"2024-2025 Academic Year applications must be submitted on or before September 7, 2024." This will ensure that your application will be considered during the first round of decisions. Subsequent applications are reviewed on a space available basis. Applications are accepted as long as places are available. Please contact the Office of Admission at 718-859-1313 to inquire about availability after the deadline. Applicants are responsible for meeting all deadlines.
