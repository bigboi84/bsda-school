Pastor Appreciation Month

Pastor Appreciation Month is a cherished occasion when congregations come together each year to show gratitude and honor their pastors and pastoral families. It's a time to recognize the tireless dedication, sacrificial efforts, and countless blessings brought forth by these exceptional individuals. Appreciating, affirming and offering prayer support to our spiritual leaders is equally important throughout the entire year.
